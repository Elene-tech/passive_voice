Revision: Infinitive or Gerund?

Quick revision of the rules you know. Try first, check the reminder if you need it.

Reminder card
  • to + verb: agree, decide, fail, manage, offer, refuse, promise, plan, afford, hope, expect, learn, tend, would like / would love; adjectives (glad, surprised, ready…) + to; too / enough + to; question words + to (not why).
  • verb + -ing: avoid, deny, enjoy, mind, postpone, suggest, consider, imagine, risk, keep, give up, can't stand, look forward to, be used to; after prepositions; go + activity.
  • bare infinitive: modals; let / make + object; hear / see / watch + object (whole action).
  • Meaning changes: stop, remember, forget, regret, try.

Revision: Time clauses

Remember: after a time conjunction we use a present form for the future.

Conjunctions: when · as · while · before · after · until / till · since · as soon as · by the time · the moment · whenever · as long as · no sooner … than · hardly … when

Comma: if the time clause comes first, use a comma. After we finish, we will wrap the bars. / We will wrap the bars after we finish.

No future after time words: I'll call you as soon as I get home (NOT will get). Use present simple or present perfect.

Main clauseTime clauseExample
present / future / imperativepresent simple or present perfectWe will wrap the bars after they have cooled.
past simple / past perfectpast simple or past perfectShe had tasted it before she changed the recipe.

How is the passive formed?

Subject + be (in the same tense as the active verb) + past participle

Add by + doer only if it is important or surprising. Drop it for people, someone, they, workers.

All the forms

FormActivePassive
Present simpleThey roast the beans.The beans are roasted.
Present continuousThey are roasting the beans.The beans are being roasted.
Past simpleThey roasted the beans.The beans were roasted.
Past continuousThey were roasting the beans.The beans were being roasted.
Present perfectThey have roasted the beans.The beans have been roasted.
Past perfectThey had roasted the beans.The beans had been roasted.
Future simpleThey will roast the beans.The beans will be roasted.
Be going toThey are going to roast the beans.The beans are going to be roasted.
Future perfectThey will have roasted the beans.The beans will have been roasted.
Present infinitiveWe will have to roast the beans again.The beans will have to be roasted again.
Perfect infinitiveShe ought to have tasted the bar.The bar ought to have been tasted.
GerundHe likes people praising his brand.He likes his brand being praised.
Perfect gerundHaving roasted the beans, they cracked them.The beans, having been roasted, were cracked.
Modal verbsThey must roast the beans. / They may have recalled the batch.The beans must be roasted. / The batch may have been recalled.
